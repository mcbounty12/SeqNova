Course Details

Course Title

Applications of Sequences and Series

A focused course that bridges pure mathematics and everyday life. Students explore how ordered lists of numbers (sequences) and their sums (series) describe real patterns of growth and decline — from populations expanding year by year to the value of assets falling over time.

Mathematics Topic

Geometric Sequences & Series

The heart of the course is the geometric sequence — a list where each term is found by multiplying the previous one by a constant ratio. Learners master the nth-term formula, finite and infinite sums, and then apply these tools to model percentage growth and reducing-balance depreciation.

Class Level

SHS 2

Designed for Senior High School Year 2 learners. The content builds on basic algebra and arithmetic sequences, then advances into geometric models with clear, step-by-step examples suitable for classroom and independent study.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  • Define and identify geometric sequences and series, and write their general terms.
  • Apply the formulas for the nth term and the sum of the first n terms of a geometric sequence.
  • Model population growth using geometric sequences and interpret the common ratio as a growth factor.
  • Model depreciation (reducing-balance method) and calculate book value after a given number of years.
  • Solve real-life problems involving growth and decay, and explain their answers clearly.
