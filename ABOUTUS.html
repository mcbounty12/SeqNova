About SeqNova

SeqNova is an educational platform dedicated to exploring the real-world applications of sequences and series. We make mathematics relatable by connecting abstract concepts with practical, everyday life situations — such as population growth and depreciation — to help learners understand the power of mathematics in shaping our world.
