Population Growth Quiz

Geometric sequences in action — growth over time

This quiz tests how well you understand population growth as a geometric sequence. You will work with the model Pn = P0 × (1 + r)n, interpret year-by-year change, find growth rates, and make simple predictions about future population size.

  • Apply the geometric growth formula
  • Calculate population after n years
  • Find the annual growth rate
  • Read and interpret growth sequences

Depreciation Quiz

Geometric decay — value falling over time

This quiz focuses on reducing-balance depreciation. You will use the model Vn = V0 × (1 − r)n to find book values, work out depreciation rates, and understand how the value of assets such as cars and equipment decreases geometrically year after year.

  • Apply the depreciation formula
  • Find book value after n years
  • Calculate the depreciation rate
  • Track value as a geometric sequence
  • Solve practical asset-value problems
  • Link decay models to real situations
